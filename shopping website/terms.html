1. Fabric Representation & Handcraft Variations

Natural handloom, handblock prints, and vegetal dyes inherently exhibit minute irregularities in yarn slubs, motif alignment, and natural shade variations. These characteristics testify to genuine human craft and are not considered manufacturing defects.

2. Selling Units & Minimum Cuts

Yardage sold by the metre is cut to order from master bolts. Fabrics ordered in multiple metres of the same SKU will be supplied as one continuous piece wherever inventory allows.

3. Pricing & Taxes

All listed retail prices are quoted in Indian Rupees (INR) and are inclusive of applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST). Wholesale quotations are provided ex-warehouse with freight and GST itemized separately.

4. Orders & Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before cutting and courier handover. Once yardage has been custom-cut or dispatched, standard return guidelines apply.
