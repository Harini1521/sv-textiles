Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or submit a wholesale enquiry at SV TEXTILES, we collect necessary contact and delivery details including your full name, email address, mobile phone number, postal PIN code, and complete shipping address. We do not store sensitive payment credentials, credit card numbers, or UPI PINs.

2. Use of Information

Your details are used solely to fulfill your orders, provide dispatch tracking, arrange courier transit, and communicate regarding quotations. We do not sell or share customer contact records with third-party advertising brokers.

3. Local Browser Storage & Cookies

We utilize local storage to persist your shopping bag items, saved wishlist selections, and active session tokens for a seamless user experience across visits.

4. Security & Firebase Rules

When integrated with Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, access to individual customer orders is restricted by strict server-side rules preventing unauthorized access.
