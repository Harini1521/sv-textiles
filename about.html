Our Heritage & Craft

Tradition Woven Into Modern Living

SV TEXTILES was founded on a simple commitment: providing authentic, high-grade Indian fabrics and garments with uncompromised transparency in yarn quality and selling units.

Honest Textile Sourcing

Rooted in Craft, Built for Modern Living

India is home to the world's richest tapestry of regional weaving clusters — from the intricate warp-and-weft tie-dyes of Telangana Ikat to the sacred temple borders of Kanchipuram and the delicate handblocks of Rajasthan.

At SV TEXTILES, we work directly with weaving cooperatives and quality-tested mills. Every textile in our store clearly states its thread count, composition, fabric width, and care recommendations. We believe our customers — whether individual boutique designers, families, or commercial retail purchasers — deserve genuine fabrics without inflated claims.
