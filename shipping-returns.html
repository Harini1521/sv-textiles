Shipping & Returns Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Shipping Coverage & Delivery Charges

SV TEXTILES delivers across all serviceable Indian PIN codes via trusted express couriers (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC).
• Orders above ₹1,999: Free Standard Delivery.
• Orders below ₹1,999: Flat shipping fee of ₹99 per consignment.

2. Dispatch & Transit Times

Orders are cut, inspected, and packed within 24 to 48 business hours. Typical transit times are 2–4 business days for major metropolitan hubs (Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad) and 4–7 business days for regional districts.

3. Returns & Replacement Criteria

• Sarees & Readymade Garments: Eligible for return within 7 calendar days of delivery provided original tags and folds remain intact and unworn.
• Fabrics Sold by the Metre: Custom-cut fabric yardage cannot be returned unless received in a damaged condition or with a demonstrable measurement shortfall.

4. Return Processing

To initiate a return or exchange, contact our customer support team at care@svtextiles.com or message our fabric desk on WhatsApp with your Order Reference ID and an unboxing photograph.
